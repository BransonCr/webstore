REQUIRED IMAGES FOR CURSED & BLESSED EMPORIUM:

1. satan.png
   - Image of Satan from South Park
   - Used to explain cursed items on product pages
   - Recommended size: 300x300 pixels
   - Format: PNG with transparent background (optional)

2. ramond.png
   - Image of Ramond the Angel
   - Used to explain blessed items on product pages
   - Recommended size: 300x300 pixels
   - Format: PNG with transparent background (optional)

Place both images in this directory (WebContent/img/)

The application will gracefully handle missing images (they won't display but won't break the page).
